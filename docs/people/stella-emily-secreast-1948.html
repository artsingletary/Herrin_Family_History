Biography

Stella Emily Secreast was born on January 13, 1948, in Macon, Bibb County, Georgia. She was the daughter of Lawrence Monroe Secreast and Margaret Fussell Secreast. Her maternal grandparents were Dr. Thomas Daniel Fussell and Sarah Redman Fussell.

The 1950 United States federal census recorded Stella, age two, living with her parents in Pensacola, Escambia County, Florida. Her father worked as a cabinet maker in a furniture shop.

Following her father's marriage to Ida Mae Herrin Sullivan on April 5, 1959, Stella became part of the extended Sullivan-Secreast family. Her stepbrothers included Michael Paul Sullivan and Billy John Sullivan. A family photograph also shows Stella alongside Ida Mae and several members of the Sullivan family.

Stella attended Savannah Technical College, where she earned an associate degree in Forest Technology. She subsequently worked at Fort Stewart, Georgia, as a forest technician and endangered species technician. During her career, she identified rare and endangered plant species at Fort Stewart and in the surrounding area.

Stella was a member of Ellabell Methodist Church and the Horticultural Society. She enjoyed botany, growing orchids, flowers and vegetables, as well as fishing and hunting.

Stella married Steve Osborn. Her obituary described Steve as her husband of 38 years at the time of her death. Her children included Ernest Coren Rahn, Donald Lee Rahn, and Sarah Emily Osborn. The dates and circumstances of any earlier marriage have not yet been established.

Stella died on February 19, 2020, at St. Joseph's Hospital, at the age of 72. Her obituary identified her husband Steve, her three children, five grandchildren, and her stepbrothers Mike Sullivan and Billy John Sullivan among her surviving family. She was preceded in death by her granddaughter Krista Breanna Rahn.

Funeral services were held on February 22, 2020, at Ellabell Methodist Church in Georgia, with burial at Bethany Campground Cemetery.
