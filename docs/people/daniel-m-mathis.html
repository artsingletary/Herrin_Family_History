Biography

Daniel M. Mathis was born on December 13, 1873, according to his World War I draft registration. He was the son of Malachi M. Matthews and Mary Williams.

Daniel married Mary Frances Darling on January 1, 1899, in Madison County, Florida. Together, they had eight children: Claude (1900–1940), Lela Mae (1902–1951), Rubin Ellis (1904–1981), Frank (1907–1969), Roy Eugene (1909–1974), Glover (1910–1973), Shirley Lee (1915–1949), and Jessie Mae Mathis (1917–1995).

The family was living in Madison County, Florida, when the 1910 United States Census was recorded. By 1918, Daniel was living in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, where he worked as a farmer.
