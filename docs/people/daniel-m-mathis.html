Biography

Daniel M. Mathis was born on December 13, 1873, according to his World War I draft registration. He married Mary Frances Darling on January 1, 1899, in Madison County, Florida. By 1918, Daniel was living in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, where he worked as a farmer.

  4. 1918 World War I Draft Registration — Daniel M. Mathis
    Type: Military

    Daniel M. Mathis registered for the World War I draft on September 12, 1918, in Lafayette County, Florida. His physical description recorded medium height, medium build, blue eyes, and dark hair. Draft registration does not establish military service.
