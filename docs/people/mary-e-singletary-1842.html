Biography

Mary E. Singletary was born in South Carolina, probably in August 1842, according to the 1900 United States Federal Census. She was a daughter of Jacob and Eliza Singletary.

Mary first appears in the 1850 United States Federal Census in Sumter District, South Carolina, in the household of Jacob and Eliza B. Singletary. She was recorded as eight years old.

By 1860, Mary was living with the Singletary household in Decatur County, Georgia. The census recorded her as fourteen years old.

In 1870, Mary E. Singletary was enumerated in District 720 of Decatur County, Georgia, in the household of Jacob Singletary. Her age was recorded as twenty-three.

By 1880, Mary had moved to Lafayette County, Florida. She was enumerated in the household headed by Eliza Singletary, along with Sarah R., John V., and Jacob Singletary. Mary was recorded as twenty-two years old. Her reported age differs substantially from the ages recorded in earlier census records.

In 1900, Mary was living in Lafayette County, Florida, in the household of William and Sarah Johnson. She was identified as William's sister-in-law, while Eliza Singletary was identified as his mother-in-law. The census recorded Mary's birth as August 1842 and her marital status as single. These household relationships provide strong evidence that Mary and Sarah were sisters and that Eliza was their mother.

Mary's whereabouts after the 1900 census have not yet been established. No confirmed death record or burial location has been identified.
