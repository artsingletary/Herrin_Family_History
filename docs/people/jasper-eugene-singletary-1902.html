Biography

Jasper Eugene Singletary was born on March 18, 1902, in Suwannee County, Florida, according to his World War II draft registration dated February 16, 1942. He was a son of Reverend Jacob A. Singletary (1852–1931) and Ida Alla Miller Singletary (1873–1945). Jasper grew up in the extended Singletary family of northern Florida, which included his siblings Eliza, Sallie, William Robert, Emma, Henry, Jamie, and Giddy Singletary.

The 1920 United States Federal Census records Jasper, age 18, living in Lafayette County, Florida, in the household of his parents, Jacob and Ida Singletary. Several of his siblings were also living in the family household at that time.

In 1927, Jasper Singletary married Amojine Landen in Suwannee County, Florida. Their marriage is recorded in the Florida Department of Health marriage index under certificate number 6071, volume 20. Jasper's 1942 World War II draft registration later identified Amogine Singletary as his next of kin, providing additional evidence of their relationship.

By April 1930, Jasper was living in the Dowling Park community of Suwannee County, Florida. He was enumerated in the household immediately preceding that of his sister Emma Singletary Evans and her husband, Willie Evans. Their consecutive census entries suggest that Jasper and Emma lived near one another during this period. The 1930 census also identifies a woman in Jasper's household whose name requires further verification.

On February 16, 1942, Jasper registered for the World War II draft in Live Oak, Suwannee County, Florida. His registration recorded his full name as Jasper Eugene Singletary, his date of birth as March 18, 1902, and his birthplace as Suwannee County, Florida. He was living in Live Oak and identified himself as his own employer. The registration named Amogine Singletary as his next of kin.

Jasper's draft registration described him as five feet eleven inches tall, weighing 150 pounds, with brown hair, brown eyes, and a ruddy complexion. Although the registration provides valuable biographical information, it does not establish that Jasper served in the military.

Jasper was named among the surviving brothers in the February 1961 obituary of William Robert Singletary and again in the April 1984 obituary and funeral memorial of his sister Emma Singletary Evans. At the time of Emma's death, Jasper was reported to be living in Live Oak, Florida. His date and place of death have not yet been established.
