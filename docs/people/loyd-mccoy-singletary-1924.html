Biography

Loyd McCoy Singletary was born on May 19, 1924, in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida. He was the son of George William Singletary and Lela Mae Mathis. Loyd grew up in the Mayo area as part of a large Singletary family that included his brothers Louie, Vershal, Dewey, and Bill, along with three sisters.

On June 30, 1942, shortly after his eighteenth birthday, Loyd registered for the military draft in Lafayette County, Florida. At the time, he was living in Mayo and listed his father, George Singletary, as his employer. His registration card described him as approximately five feet nine inches tall, weighing 115 pounds, with blue eyes and brown hair.

Loyd entered the United States Navy in May 1943 during World War II. A Navy muster roll records his enlistment on May 18, 1943, at Jacksonville, Florida, although a separate military index reports May 25, 1943.

On November 15, 1943, Loyd was received aboard USS Darby (DE-218), a destroyer escort, after assignment at the Naval Training Station in Norfolk, Virginia. At that time, his rating was Seaman Second Class. Another military record identifies him with USS Darby on October 1, 1945, documenting his association with the vessel at two points during the war.

Loyd served in the United States Naval Reserve and attained the rating of Seaman First Class, as recorded on his military gravestone. He was discharged from the Navy on November 26, 1945.

On February 10, 1946, Loyd married Nina Mae Phillips in Echols County, Georgia. Their marriage took place less than three months after his discharge from the Navy. The couple had four children: Fay, Derenda, Paul, and Joe.

Following World War II, Loyd settled with his family in Jacksonville, Florida. By 1958, he was employed as a milkman for Sealtest, a dairy company. His death certificate records his residence in Jacksonville at the time of his death.

Loyd died on January 17, 1958, at Duval Medical Center in Jacksonville, Florida. His death certificate identifies the cause as carcinoma of the lung with metastases. He was only 33 years old.

His funeral was held on January 19, 1958, at Airline Baptist Church near Mayo, Florida, with arrangements handled by Joe P. Burns Funeral Home. He was buried in the adjoining Airline Baptist Church Cemetery in Lafayette County, Florida.

Loyd's military gravestone commemorates his service during World War II with the inscription S1 USNR, identifying him as a Seaman First Class in the United States Naval Reserve. His obituary appeared in The Florida Times-Union on January 19, 1958.
