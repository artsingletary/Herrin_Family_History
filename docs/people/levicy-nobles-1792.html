Biography

Levicy Nobles was born in Georgia in 1792, the daughter of Lewis Sanders Nobles, who is identified as a Revolutionary War soldier in Pioneers of Wiregrass Georgia. The name of her mother has not yet been established in the records collected for this archive.

On April 19, 1810, Levicy married David Sears (1791–1879) in Montgomery County, Georgia. Their marriage is documented in a historical marriage record and is also recorded in Pioneers of Wiregrass Georgia.

Levicy and David raised thirteen children: Luraney, Charity, Hiram, Lavina, Harriet, Matilda, George W., Charlotte, Mary, Tobitha, Benjamin Franklin, Thaddeus, and David. Their children were born between approximately 1811 and 1830.

Among their children was Charity Sears (1812–1898), who married Levi Herrin (1804–1890). Through Charity, Levicy became an ancestor of both branches of the Herrin family that eventually reunited through the marriage of William Levi Herrin and Laura Etta Palmer in April 1916.

Around 1825, the Sears family moved from Montgomery County to what was then Ware County, Georgia, settling in the 586th Georgia Militia District. The area later became part of Clinch, Coffee, and Atkinson counties as county boundaries changed.

Levicy lived through a period of considerable change in southern Georgia, including the years leading up to the American Civil War. She died in 1868, three years after the war ended.

Levicy and David were members of Union Hill Congregational Church and were buried in its cemetery near present-day Axson, Atkinson County, Georgia.
