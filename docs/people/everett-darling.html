Biography

Everett Darling was born April 14, 1854, in Connecticut. According to a family account provided by his granddaughter Bettie Darling Prestridge, Everett experienced a difficult childhood. He was reportedly given away as an infant and raised in poorhouses and foster homes. His biological parents have not yet been conclusively identified.

Bettie related a story passed down through her father, James Darling, concerning Everett's discovery of his mother's identity. In 1875, Everett reportedly learned that a woman he had known for many years was actually his biological mother. Upset by this discovery, he decided to leave Connecticut and begin a new life in Florida.

According to the family story, Everett traveled by ship to Jacksonville, Florida, arriving on April 14, 1875, his twenty-first birthday. He subsequently moved inland to Madison County, Florida, where he established his home.

In 1879, Everett married Sarah Wadsworth Blanton, a widow who had children from her previous marriage. Everett and Sarah had two daughters together, Mary and Martha Darling. Their daughter Mary Frances Darling became an ancestor of the Singletary family.

Everett and Sarah remained married until Sarah's death in 1908. The following year, in 1909, Everett married Susan Lucinda Nipper. They had four children: William Everett Darling, born in 1911; Nettie Darling, born in 1914; James Darling, born in 1917; and John Jackson Darling, born in 1920.

Everett spent the remainder of his life in Florida. He died September 11, 1935, in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, at the age of 81.

Bettie Darling Prestridge also cautioned against confusing her grandfather with another Everett Darling who appears in New England census records as the son of Moses Darling and Caroline Sharp. According to Bettie's research, that Everett was born in Massachusetts and remained in New England, while her grandfather was born in Connecticut and eventually settled in Florida. The parentage of Everett Darling of Mayo, Florida, therefore remains an unresolved genealogical question.
