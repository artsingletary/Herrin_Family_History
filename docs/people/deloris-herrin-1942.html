Sources

  1. 1945 Florida State Census — William Levi Herrin Family
    Type: Census

    The 1945 Florida State Population Census records the household of William Levi Herrin (listed as W. L. Herrin) and his wife Laura Etta Herrin, living on Route 2 near Mayo in Precinct 3, Lafayette County, Florida. Their household includes seven children: James, Gladys, Mary Lou, Dorothy, Billy, Evelyn, and Deloris. Evelyn was recorded as age 10 and attending school. The youngest child is identified through family research as Deloris Herrin, although her name appears to have been recorded differently, possibly as 'Dean,' in the original census. Immediately preceding the Herrin household is the household of Louie Singletary, brother of Dewey Singletary. Louie's household includes his wife Lessie and their young son Randall. The consecutive entries document the presence of both branches of the family in the Mayo area in 1945.

    Location: Precinct 3, Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, United States

    Household: William Levi Herrin Household

    Household Members:

    • W. L. Herrin, age 52, laborer
    • Laura Herrin, age 46, housewife
    • James Herrin, age 24, Army
    • Gladys Herrin, age 20, laborer
    • Mary Lou Herrin, age 17, student
    • Dorothy Herrin, age 14, student
    • Billy Herrin, age 12, student
    • Evelyn Herrin, age 10, student
    • Deloris Herrin, age 6 (name appears as 'Dean' or similar in the original census)
