Biography

Olive Herrin was reportedly born in North Carolina around 1793. According to family research, she was a daughter of Heron Herrin and Ann D.

Olive reportedly married Moses Turner in 1810. Family research indicates that the couple moved to Hamilton County, Florida, following their marriage.

Olive is reported to have died in Wayne County, Georgia, around 1830. These details remain to be verified against historical records.
