Biography

Ann D., whose maiden name has not been established, was the wife of Heron Herrin. Family research identifies her as the mother of seven children born between approximately 1793 and 1810.

Following Heron's death in 1817, Ann reportedly married Levi J. Knight. According to family research, she later lived in Wayne County, Georgia, where she died around 1830.
