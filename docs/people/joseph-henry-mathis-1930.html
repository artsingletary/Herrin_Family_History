Biography

Joseph Henry Mathis was born April 19, 1930, in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, the son of Frank Mathis and Mary Virginia Allen. He was a brother of Honorine Virginia Mathis.

Joseph served in the United States military and held the rank of Specialist. His branch of service and dates of service have not yet been established.

Joseph was living in Albany, Georgia, when his father's obituary was published in 1969.

He died March 12, 2012, in Albany, Dougherty County, Georgia, at age 81. He was buried at Andersonville National Cemetery in Andersonville, Macon County, Georgia.
