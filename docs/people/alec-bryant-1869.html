Biography

Alec Bryant was born around 1869. In the 1880 United States Federal Census, Alec, age 11, was living with his mother, Dolly Bryant, and siblings Myram, Hattie, and Mary in Columbia County, Florida.

Sources

  1. 1880 United States Federal Census — Dolly Bryant Household
    Type: census

    1880 United States Federal Census, Columbia County, Florida, Enumeration District 67, page 21 (stamped page 395), lines 41–45. The household is headed by Dolly Bryant, age 40, and includes her children Myram, age 20, Alec, age 11, Hattie, age 7, and Mary, age 5. Family research identifies Dolly as Dolly Ann Leslie, widow of John Waters Bryant, who died in 1878. The census documents the Bryant family's residence in Columbia County, Florida, and places Mary Eveline Bryant's approximate birth year around 1874–1875.
