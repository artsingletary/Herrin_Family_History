Sources

  1. 1920 U.S. Federal Census — Daniel M. Mathis Family
    Type: Census

    1920 United States Federal Census, Lafayette County, Florida, Precinct No. 3, sheet 7B, lines 86–96. The household includes Dan M. Mathis (47), wife Mary (37), and their eight children: Claude (19), Lela (17), Rube (15), Frank (13), Roy (11), Glover (10), Shirley (6), and Jessie M. (2). Also residing in the household was Ella Truluck (15), identified as a niece. Ella was the daughter of Silas P. Truluck and Annie E. Mathis, Daniel's sister and a daughter of Malachi M. Mathis and Mary Williams.

  2. 1930 U.S. Federal Census — Daniel M. Mathis Family
    Type: census

    1930 United States Federal Census, Lafayette County, Florida, Election Precinct No. 3, Arline, Enumeration District 34-4, sheet 1A, lines 1–7, enumerated April 2, 1930. The household includes Daniel M. Mathis (56), wife Mary F. Mathis (46), sons Claude E. (29) and Roy E. (20), daughters Shirley L. (13) and Jessie M. (11), and Daniel's father-in-law Everett Darling (79). Everett's presence provides additional evidence that Mary Frances Mathis was his daughter. Daniel, Claude, and Roy were recorded as farm workers or farmers.

  3. 1935 Florida State Census — Daniel M. Mathis Family
    Type: census

    1935 Florida State Census, Lafayette County, Precinct No. 2. The census lists D. M. Mathis (62), Emma F. Mathis (54), Claude Mathis (35), Roy Mathis (25), Shirley Mathis (19), Jessie Mathis (17), E. Darling (81), Joseph Mathis (5), and Honorine Mathis (2). E. Darling is identified as a boarder and is believed to be Everett Darling, father of Mary Frances Darling Mathis. Joseph Henry Mathis and Honorine Virginia Mathis were children of Daniel and Mary's son Frank Mathis. The name Emma F. Mathis should be compared with other records identifying Daniel's wife as Mary Frances Mathis.
