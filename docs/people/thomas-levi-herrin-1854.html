Biography

Thomas Levi Herrin married Elizabeth Branch on February 11, 1878, in Ware County, Georgia. Their marriage was performed by Thomas Brabham, a preacher of the Gospel. The original marriage certificate records his surname as Herring. Elizabeth was Thomas's first wife. He later married Nancy T. Jewell, with whom he had his son William Levi Herrin (1892–1975).
