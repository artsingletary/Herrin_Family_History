Biography

James M. Lesley was born about 1809 in Georgia. He is a probable father of Dolly Ann Leslie Bryan, based on the 1850 United States Census.

In September 1850, James was living in District 68 of Pike County, Georgia, with Elizabeth Lesley and five younger household members, including D. A. Lesley, age 15.

James was employed as a brick mason and reported real estate valued at $1,000.

The household is believed to represent James, his wife Elizabeth, and their children. However, the 1850 census did not record family relationships, so these connections remain provisional.
