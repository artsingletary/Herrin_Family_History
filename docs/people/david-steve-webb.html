Biography

David Steve Webb, known as Stevie, is the son of David Brewer Webb (1941–2007) and Evelyn Herrin (1934–2011).

Stevie grew up in the Taylor County, Florida, area and appears in a 1988 Taylor County High School yearbook index.

He is a maternal half-brother of Connie Ray Mathis and Richard (Ricky) Mathis.
