Biography

Jacob A. Singletary was born on March 15, 1852, a son of Jacob and Eliza Singletary. His gravestone records this birth date, and his Florida death certificate records March 1852, although the handwritten day is somewhat difficult to distinguish. His birthplace remains uncertain.

Jacob appears in the 1860 United States Federal Census in Decatur County, Georgia, in the household of Jacob and Eliza Singletary. He was recorded as approximately seven years old.

In 1870, Jacob was enumerated in Decatur County, Georgia, in the household of Jacob Singletary.

By 1880, Jacob was living in Lafayette County, Florida, in the household headed by Eliza Singletary. He was recorded as twenty years old.

Jacob became a minister and was identified as Reverend Jacob Singletary on his gravestone. Elder J. A. Singletary performed the marriage ceremony of William Johnson and Sarah R. Singletary on January 30, 1890, in Lafayette County, Florida. This was probably Reverend Jacob A. Singletary, although the identification has not yet been conclusively established.

On April 16, 1890, Jacob A. Singletary married Ida Alla Miller in Lafayette County, Florida. Their original marriage records identify Redden B. Hill as the county judge who issued the license and D. H. Ward as the person who performed the marriage ceremony. The license was filed for record on April 26, 1890. Ida's name appears as Ida Alla Miller in the marriage record and as Ally Ida Singletary on their shared gravestone.

Jacob's Florida death certificate identifies his occupation as farmer. He died on May 14, 1931, in Lafayette County, Florida. The certificate records heart trouble as his cause of death.

Reverend Jacob Singletary was buried at Bell's Mill Creek Cemetery, also known as Bethel Creek Baptist Church Cemetery, in Day, Lafayette County, Florida. His gravestone also commemorates his wife, Ally Ida Singletary, who died on October 16, 1945.
