Biography

An Eliza Branch, age two, appears in the 1860 United States Census in Ware County, Georgia, in the household of Henry and Mary Branch. Mary Branch may have been Mary Ann Adkinson, born about 1837, who appears in the 1850 census of Laurens County, Georgia. The identification of these individuals as Elizabeth Branch's parents and maternal grandparents remains unconfirmed.
