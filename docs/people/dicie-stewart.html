Biography

Dicie Stewart married Albert Eugene Singletary on August 31, 1919, in Lafayette County, Florida, according to a Florida marriage record.

Albert's 1928 Florida death certificate identifies his wife with a name previously read as Daisy Stewart. The marriage record identifies her as Dicie Stewart. Further examination of the original records may help explain the discrepancy.

Additional information about Dicie's birth, parents, and later life has not yet been established.
