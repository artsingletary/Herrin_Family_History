Biography

Mary Frances Darling was born on June 4, 1881. She married Daniel M. Mathis on January 1, 1899, in Madison County, Florida.

Daniel and Mary Frances had eight children: Claude, Lela Mae, Rubin Ellis, Frank, Roy Eugene, Glover, Shirley Lee, and Jessie Mae Mathis. The family was living in Madison County, Florida, when the 1910 United States Census was recorded.
