Biography

Elizabeth Lesley was born about 1812 in Georgia. Her maiden surname has not yet been established.

The 1850 United States Census records Elizabeth, age 38, living in District 68 of Pike County, Georgia, in the household of James M. Lesley.

The household included D. A. Lesley, age 15, who is believed to be Dolly Ann Leslie, the future wife of John Waters Bryan.

Elizabeth and James are considered probable parents of Dolly Ann Leslie, although additional documentation is needed to confirm the relationship.
