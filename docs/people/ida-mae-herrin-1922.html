Biography

Ida Mae Herrin, affectionately known as Shorty, was born on January 2, 1922, in Homerville, Clinch County, Georgia. She was the daughter of William Levi Herrin and Lauretta (Laura Etta) Palmer Herrin. Her gravestone records her birth year as 1922, while a Social Security application and claims record gives January 2, 1923. This discrepancy has not yet been resolved.

On April 17, 1942, Ida Mae married Hope Sullivan in Lafayette County, Florida. Their marriage is documented in a Florida marriage index. Eight children with the Sullivan surname, or a married variation of it, are listed on Ida Mae's later gravestone: Stella S. Osborn, Nell Sullivan, Connie Mack Sullivan, Billy Sullivan, Wayland Sullivan, Mike Sullivan, Jack Sullivan, and Drew Sullivan. Their individual parentage and birth dates have not yet been independently verified.

On April 5, 1959, Ida Mae married Lawrence M. Secreast. A Florida marriage index records the marriage in Wakulla County in April 1959, and their shared gravestone supplies the exact date. Lawrence was born on February 18, 1912, and died on June 3, 1982.

Ida Mae died on July 14, 2003, in Fanning Springs, Gilchrist County, Florida, at the age of 81. She was buried at Old Town Memorial Cemetery in Old Town, Dixie County, Florida, alongside her husband Lawrence M. Secreast. Their shared gravestone commemorates their marriage on April 5, 1959, and lists eight children.
