Biography

Myrtis Irene Hurst, also identified as Myrtis I. Jackson in her obituary, died on January 31, 2012, at age 81. She was a resident of Holiday, Florida. Her obituary noted that she was preceded in death by her son James T. Herrin and survived by four children: William, Diana, Patricia, and Janet, as well as numerous grandchildren and great-grandchildren. Funeral services were arranged through Sylvan Abbey Memorial Park & Funeral Home in Clearwater, Florida, for February 3, 2012. Source: Tampa Bay Times, February 2, 2012.
