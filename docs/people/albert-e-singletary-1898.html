Biography

Albert Eugene Singletary was born in October 1898, according to the 1900 United States Federal Census. His 1928 Florida death certificate instead records his birth year as 1899 and identifies his birthplace as Lafayette County, Florida.

Albert was the son of John V. Singletary and Mary Bryant (Bryan). In 1900, he was living with his parents and siblings Jessie, Annie, and George in Lafayette County, Florida.

Albert worked as a farmer in Lafayette County. He was married to Dicie Stewart on August 31, 1919, in Lafayette County, according to a Florida marriage record.

Albert died in November 1928 at approximately 29 years of age from an accidental gunshot wound to the abdomen. His death certificate identifies the cause of death as an accidental gunshot wound and records his burial location as Midway Cemetery in Lafayette County, Florida.

Albert's full name was Albert Eugene Singletary. The 1900 Federal Census records his middle initial as E., while his death certificate appears to record L. This discrepancy has not yet been resolved.

Albert's death certificate identifies his father as John V. Singletary, born in South Carolina, and his mother as Mary Brian, born in Florida. The 1900 census records different parental birthplaces, so these details require further investigation.
