Biography

Jacob Singletary was born in South Carolina around 1800–1804, according to information recorded in the United States federal censuses of 1850, 1860, and 1870. His exact birth date and the identities of his parents have not been established.

In 1850, Jacob was living in Sumter District, South Carolina, where the federal census recorded him as fifty years old and employed as a farmer. His household included Eliza B. Singletary, age thirty-five, and six younger family members: Silas P., Sarah R., Mary E., George M., Jane H., and Samuel J. Singletary.

By 1860, Jacob and his family had relocated to the 19th District of Decatur County, Georgia. The federal census recorded Jacob as fifty-six years old and born in South Carolina. His household included Eliza, Silas, Sarah, Mary, George, Harrietta, Samuel, John, and a younger Jacob Singletary. The birthplaces recorded for the younger household members suggest that the family moved from South Carolina to Georgia during the early 1850s.

The 1860 census included John Singletary, age eight, born in Georgia. John also appeared in Jacob's household in 1870. These records provide strong evidence connecting John Vershal Singletary to Jacob's family, although the censuses themselves do not explicitly identify their relationship.

In 1870, Jacob was living in District 720, also identified as the Jones District, in Decatur County, Georgia. The federal census recorded him as sixty-nine years old and born in South Carolina. His household included Elizabeth Singletary, Silas, Rachel, Sarah A., Mary E., John, and a younger Jacob Singletary.

Jacob's whereabouts after 1870 have not been established. By 1880, Eliza Singletary was living in Lafayette County, Florida, as head of a household that included John V. Singletary and other family members. Jacob's exact date and place of death remain unknown.
