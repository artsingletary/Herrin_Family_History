Sources

  1. 1885 Florida State Census — Everett Darling Family
    Type: census

    1885 Florida State Census, Madison County, Florida, documenting the household of Everett Darling and his wife Sarah Wadsworth. Lydia, Everett's stepdaughter, is enumerated immediately below Sarah, followed by their daughters Mary and Martha Darling. Lydia's biological father has not been identified. This census provides evidence of the blended Darling-Wadsworth household.

  2. 1900 U.S. Census — George and Martha Simpson
    Type: census

    1900 United States Census, Madison County, Florida, enumerated June 23, 1900. George Simpson and his wife Martha appear on lines 83–84. George is recorded as age 23, born December 1876. Martha's birth date appears to be September 1882, consistent with her later death certificate and Social Security records, although her recorded age may differ slightly. The census identifies them as a married couple.

    View Document

  3. 1937 Social Security NUMIDENT Record — Martha Darling Simpson
    Type: other

    United States Social Security Numerical Identification Files (NUMIDENT), 1936–2007. Record for Martha Darling Simpson, with alias Martha Darling, documenting a May 1937 Social Security application. The record gives her birth date as September 14, 1882, in Madison County, Florida, and identifies her parents as Everett Darling and Sarah L. Wadsworth. Her Florida death certificate instead records September 9, 1882, and identifies her father as Jim Darling.

  4. 1950 Florida Death Index — Martha Dowling Simpson
    Type: death

    Florida Death Index entry for Martha Dowling Simpson, who died in Suwannee County, Florida, in 1950. Certificate number 27485, volume 1443, file FDI03-1950.txt, line 24264. The entry corresponds to her Florida death certificate.

  5. 1950 Florida Death Certificate — Martha Darling Simpson
    Type: death

    Florida death certificate, state file number 27485, documenting the death of Martha Darling Simpson on December 18, 1950, in Live Oak, Suwannee County, Florida. Born September 9, 1882, in Madison County, Florida, she was widowed at the time of her death. The certificate names her father as Jim Darling and lists her mother's maiden name as unknown. She was buried at Philadelphia Cemetery on December 20, 1950.
