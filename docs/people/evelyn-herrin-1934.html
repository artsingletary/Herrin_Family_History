Biography

Evelyn Herrin was born on September 14, 1934, in Pearson, Atkinson County, Georgia.

In 1945, Evelyn was living with her parents, William Levi and Laura Etta Herrin, on Route 2 near Mayo in Lafayette County, Florida. The Florida State Census recorded Evelyn as a ten-year-old student living with several of her brothers and sisters. A surviving school portrait from the 1950–1951 school year further documents her connection to Mayo during her teenage years.

Evelyn spent part of her youth in Lafayette County, Florida. A surviving school portrait from the 1950–1951 school year identifies Mayo, Florida, as the location of her school photograph.

She was a daughter of William Levi Herrin and Laura Etta Palmer. She died on February 19, 2011, in Florida.

During her lifetime, Evelyn married Freddy Ray Mathis (1938–1967), John S. George, and David Brewer Webb (1942–2007). Florida divorce records document her divorce from John S. George in Pinellas County in October 1968 and from David Webb in Taylor County on June 6, 1975.

Evelyn died on February 19, 2011, in Perry, Taylor County, Florida, at the age of 76. She is buried at Waters Memorial Gardens in Steinhatchee, Florida, where a shared memorial commemorates her and David 'Buddy' Webb (1941–2007). The marker also names Stevie, Ricky, Connie Ray, and Debra as their children.

  2. 1945 Florida State Census — William Levi Herrin Family
    Type: Census

    The 1945 Florida State Population Census records the household of William Levi Herrin (listed as W. L. Herrin) and his wife Laura Etta Herrin, living on Route 2 near Mayo in Precinct 3, Lafayette County, Florida. Their household includes seven children: James, Gladys, Mary Lou, Dorothy, Billy, Evelyn, and Deloris. Evelyn was recorded as age 10 and attending school. The youngest child is identified through family research as Deloris Herrin, although her name appears to have been recorded differently, possibly as 'Dean,' in the original census. Immediately preceding the Herrin household is the household of Louie Singletary, brother of Dewey Singletary. Louie's household includes his wife Lessie and their young son Randall. The consecutive entries document the presence of both branches of the family in the Mayo area in 1945.

    Location: Precinct 3, Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, United States

    Household: William Levi Herrin Household

    Household Members:

    • W. L. Herrin, age 52, laborer
    • Laura Herrin, age 46, housewife
    • James Herrin, age 24, Army
    • Gladys Herrin, age 20, laborer
    • Mary Lou Herrin, age 17, student
    • Dorothy Herrin, age 14, student
    • Billy Herrin, age 12, student
    • Evelyn Herrin, age 10, student
    • Deloris Herrin, age 6 (name appears as 'Dean' or similar in the original census)
