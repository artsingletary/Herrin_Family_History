Biography

Mary Williams married Malachi M. Matthews on October 27, 1870, in Madison County, Florida.

The transcription of the Matthews family Bible records her death as July 16, 1903. Her birth year remains uncertain because the Bible transcription and the 1885 Florida Census provide conflicting information.
