Biography

Mary Williams married Malachi M. Matthews on October 27, 1870, in Madison County, Florida.

The transcription of the Matthews family Bible records her death as July 16, 1903. Her birth year remains uncertain because the Bible transcription and the 1885 Florida Census provide conflicting information.

    Type: Marriage

    Madison County marriage register, page 139, upper-right entry, documenting the marriage of Malachi Matthews and Mary Williams on October 27, 1870.   2. 1880 United States Census - Mack Mathis Household
    Type: census

    1880 United States Census, Madison County, Florida, District 7, enumerated June 16, 1880, page 19, lines 22–26. The household lists Mack Mathis, age 34; his wife Mary, age 28; and their children Daniel, age 8, Mary Jane, age 7, and John, age 1. The family surname appears as Mathis, a spelling variation also found as Matthews in other family records.

  3. 1885 Florida State Census - Malachi Mathis Household
    Type: census

    1885 Florida State Census, Madison County, Florida, page 6, enumerated June 21, 1885. The household includes Malachi Mathis, his wife Mary, and children including Daniel, John, Annie, and William. Annie was recorded as age two and William as five months old. The family's reported ages and names should be compared with other census records.

  4. 1900 United States Census - Mac Mathes Household
    Type: census

    1900 United States Census, Madison County, Florida, Precinct 3, enumerated June 22, 1900, lines 60–64. The household lists Mac Mathes, born May 1844, age 56; wife Mary, born May 1861, age 39; son John, born October 1879, age 20; daughter Annie, born September 1882, age 17; and son Willie, born in 1890, age nine. The surname appears as Mathes, another spelling associated with the Mathis/Matthews family. The identity of Willie in relation to William recorded in the 1885 census requires further investigation.
