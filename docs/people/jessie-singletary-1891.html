Biography

Jessie Singletary was born in March 1891 in Florida, according to the 1900 United States Federal Census. He was the son of John V. Singletary and Mary Bryant Singletary.

In 1900, Jessie was living with his parents and siblings in Lafayette County, Florida.

Jessie does not appear in his parents' household in the 1910 Federal Census. Family research suggests that he may have died young, possibly around the time of his parents' deaths, but no death record has yet been located to establish when or where he died.
