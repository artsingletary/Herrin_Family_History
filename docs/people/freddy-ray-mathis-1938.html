Biography

Freddy Ray Mathis was born around 1938 and died on December 19, 1967, at the age of 29.

He was married to Evelyn Herrin, daughter of William Levi Herrin and Laura Etta Palmer.

His obituary, published in The Florida Times-Union on December 21, 1967, identifies two sons, Connie and Ricky, and a stepdaughter, Mrs. Debra George.

Funeral services were held on December 21, 1967, with burial at Woodlawn Cemetery.
