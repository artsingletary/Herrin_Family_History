Biography

Dolly Ann Leslie was born in Georgia, probably during the mid-1830s. Historical census records give varying ages, suggesting birth years between approximately 1834 and 1840.

Dolly married John W. Bryan on January 2, 1853, in Spalding County, Georgia. Their original marriage record confirms the date and location.

The 1860 United States Census records Dolly, age 24, living with John and several children in Spalding County, Georgia. The family remained in Spalding County in 1870.

Following John's death in December 1878, Dolly appears as a widow in the 1880 United States Census in Columbia County, Florida. Her household included her daughter Mary Bryant, age five, along with other children.

A Savannah, Georgia, death record documents the death of Mrs. D. A. Bryan on June 6, 1906, at Telfair Hospital. The record gives her age as 72 and identifies Laurel Grove as her burial location. A Find a Grave memorial identifies this woman as Dolly Ann Leslie Bryan.

Dolly is memorialized at Laurel Grove Cemetery North in Savannah, Chatham County, Georgia. Her Find a Grave memorial identifies plot 0-0-1110-X6.

The 1850 United States Census records a 15-year-old D. A. Lesley living with James M. Lesley and Elizabeth Lesley in Pike County, Georgia. This individual is believed to be Dolly Ann Leslie, who married John W. Bryan in 1853. James and Elizabeth are therefore considered Dolly's probable parents, although the relationship has not been independently confirmed.
