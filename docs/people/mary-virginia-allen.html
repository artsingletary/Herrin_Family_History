Biography

Mary Virginia Allen was born May 4, 1911, in Lafayette County, Florida, the daughter of Lewis Allen and Florence Fryer.

Mary married Frank Mathis (1907–1969). They had two children, Joseph Henry Mathis, born April 19, 1930, and Honorine Virginia Mathis, born October 5, 1932.

Mary died June 7, 1933, in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, at the age of 21. Her death certificate identifies Frank Mathis as her husband.

At the time of Mary's death, Joseph was three years old and Honorine was approximately eight months old.
