Biography

Silas P. Singletary was born in South Carolina, probably in March 1835. Both the 1900 United States Federal Census and a burial record report his birth as March 1835. However, federal census records from 1850, 1860, and 1870 consistently suggest a birth around 1839. His exact birth date has not been conclusively established.

In 1850, Silas was living in Sumter District, South Carolina, in the household of Jacob and Eliza B. Singletary. The federal census recorded him as eleven years old.

By 1860, Silas had moved with the Singletary household to Decatur County, Georgia. The census recorded him as twenty-one years old and born in South Carolina. The household included Jacob, Eliza, and several younger family members.

During the American Civil War, Silas P. Singletary served in the Confederate Army. The National Archives' Index to Compiled Service Records of Confederate Soldiers from Georgia (publication M226) identifies an S. P. Singletary as a private in Company A of the 59th Georgia Infantry. His exact dates of military service have not been established. A Confederate widow's pension application filed by Rachel Griffin Singletary in Thomas County, Georgia, in 1916 provides additional evidence connecting this soldier to her husband, Silas P. Singletary. The original compiled military service records have not yet been examined.

On October 14, 1869, Silas married Rachel Griffin in Decatur County, Georgia. The original county marriage register records the groom's name as Simon P. Singletary, indicating that he may also have been known by that name. The marriage was performed by J. W. Oliver, a minister of the gospel. Rachel's later Confederate widow's pension application also identifies her husband as S. P. Singletary and reports their marriage in October 1869.

In 1870, Silas remained in Decatur County, Georgia, where he was enumerated in the household of Jacob Singletary. He was recorded as thirty-one years old. Rachel Griffin, whom he had married the previous year, was also recorded in the household.

By June 1900, Silas was living in Thomas County, Georgia, with his wife Rachel and four children: Emma P., Mary, Robert, and Dollie. The census recorded his birth as March 1835, consistent with the information reported in his burial record.

Silas P. Singletary died on January 10, 1915, according to a burial record. He was buried at Springhill Christian Church Cemetery in Ochlocknee, Thomas County, Georgia.

Following Silas's death, his widow, Rachel Griffin Singletary, applied for a Confederate widow's pension in Thomas County, Georgia. The application, dated in 1916, identifies her husband as S. P. Singletary and provides information about their marriage and his military service. The application also contains a handwritten statement concerning differences in recollections of the couple's marriage. Their marriage date is independently documented in the original Decatur County marriage register dated October 14, 1869.
