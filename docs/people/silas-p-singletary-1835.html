Biography

Silas P. Singletary was born in South Carolina during the 1830s. A burial record reports his birth as March 1835, while federal census records from 1850, 1860, and 1870 consistently suggest a birth around 1839. His exact birth date has not been conclusively established.

In 1850, Silas P. Singletary was living in Sumter District, South Carolina, in the household of Jacob and Eliza B. Singletary. The federal census recorded him as eleven years old.

By 1860, Silas had moved with the Singletary household to Decatur County, Georgia. The census recorded him as twenty-one years old and born in South Carolina. The household included Jacob, Eliza, and several younger family members.

On October 14, 1869, Silas P. Singletary married Rachel Griffin in Decatur County, Georgia. The original county marriage register records the groom's name as Simon P. Singletary, suggesting that he was also known by that name. The marriage was performed by J. W. Oliver, a minister of the gospel. Later census records identify Rachel as Silas's wife.

In 1870, Silas remained in Decatur County, Georgia, where he was enumerated in Jacob Singletary's household. He was recorded as thirty-one years old, again suggesting a birth around 1839.

A burial record reports that Silas P. Singletary died on January 10, 1915, and was buried at Springhill Christian Church Cemetery in Ochlocknee, Thomas County, Georgia. The burial record gives his birth as March 1835, which differs from the ages recorded in the earlier federal censuses.

In June 1900, Silas Singletary was living in Thomas County, Georgia, with his wife Rachel and four children: Emma P., Mary, Robert, and Dollie. The census recorded his birth as March 1835, consistent with the birth information reported in his burial record. Earlier census records, however, suggest a later birth year.
