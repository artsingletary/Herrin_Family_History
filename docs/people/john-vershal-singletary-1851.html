Biography

John Vershal Singletary was born in Georgia, probably during the early 1850s. The 1900 United States Federal Census records his birth as August 1851, although his reported age varies considerably among census records. Family research identifies his parents as Jacob and Eliza Singletary. John spent his early years in southern Georgia before settling in Lafayette County, Florida.

In 1870, John was living in Decatur County, Georgia, in a household headed by Jacob Singletary. The census recorded John as sixteen years old and identified his occupation as farm laborer. Other members of the household included Elizabeth Singletary and several younger family members. The household provides an important connection between John's early life in Georgia and the Singletary family that later settled in northern Florida.

By June 1880, John was living in Lafayette County, Florida. The United States Federal Census, enumerated on June 25, listed Eliza Singletary as head of household, age fifty-four. Also living in the household were Sarah Singletary, age twenty-four; Mary E. Singletary, age twenty-two; John V. Singletary, age twenty-one; and Jacob Singletary, age twenty. Family research identifies these younger household members as John's siblings. The census establishes that John and members of his family had settled in Lafayette County by 1880.

John established himself as a farmer in Lafayette County. In 1882, the Florida State Gazetteer listed him among the county's prominent farmers and recorded that he owned forty acres of land in the Bula community. The Bula post office was located at Irvin's Ferry on the Old Luraville Road, approximately four miles from Mayo, Florida.

On February 4, 1890, John married Mary Bryant in Lafayette County, Florida. Their marriage license had been issued on February 3, and the ceremony was performed by Reverend A. T. Wood. The marriage was recorded on March 3, 1890, in Lafayette County Marriage Record Book B, page 422. Mary's surname appears as both Bryant and Bryan in historical and family records. She was born in Florida in May 1875, according to the 1900 census.

John and Mary raised a family in Lafayette County. Their children included Jessie Von Singletary, Annie Evaline Singletary, George William Singletary, Albert Eugene Singletary, Alice Geneva Singletary, and Viola Singletary. The 1900 United States Federal Census recorded John as a farmer living with Mary and their four oldest children: Jessie, Annie, George, and Albert. The census reported that John was born in August 1851 and that the couple had been married for ten years.

The family continued living in Lafayette County during the early twentieth century. By the time of the 1910 Federal Census, John and Mary had six children, including their younger daughters Alice and Viola. John's reported age in the 1910 census differs from his age in earlier records, leaving his exact birth year uncertain.

John's later years and exact date of death have not been established. He and Mary appear in the 1910 Federal Census, but their circumstances after that time remain uncertain. In January 1920, their son George William Singletary, then twenty-four years old, was living in Lafayette County with his wife Lela, their infant son Dewey, and George's younger sisters Alice and Viola. Alice was seventeen years old and Viola was fourteen. Their presence in George's household suggests that he had assumed responsibility for helping care for his younger siblings, although the circumstances leading to this arrangement are unknown.

John Vershal Singletary and Mary Evline Bryant Singletary are buried at Bethel Creek Baptist Church Cemetery, formerly known as Bell's Mill Creek Cemetery, in Day, Lafayette County, Florida. Their original grave markers remain at the cemetery. In 2011, an additional shared headstone was installed by their great-grandson, Arthur Singletary. The death dates inscribed on that memorial reflect family research and have not been independently verified. John's exact date of death therefore remains unknown.

A cemetery survey conducted in May 1968 by Moseley and Verrill at Bethel Creek Baptist Church Cemetery in Lafayette County, Florida, recorded John V. Singletary's gravestone dates as 1859–1911. A photograph of the original gravestone taken in spring 2026 shows a heavily weathered inscription, although the birth year 1859 remains partially legible. The 1968 transcription provides important evidence that John died in 1911. His recorded birth year of 1859 conflicts with earlier census records, particularly the 1860 census, which establishes that he was already living before 1859. The 1900 census records his birth as August 1851. His probable birth year is therefore retained as 1851, while 1911 is provisionally accepted as his death year.
