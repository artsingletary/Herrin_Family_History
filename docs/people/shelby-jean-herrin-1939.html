Biography

Shelby Jean Herrin was born February 26, 1939, in Clinch County, Georgia. She was a daughter of William Levi Herrin and Laura Etta Palmer.

Shelby married Arthur M. Freeman and lived in Stockbridge, Henry County, Georgia. Her occupation was recorded as key punch operator.

Shelby died September 12, 1978, at Clayton General Hospital in Riverdale, Clayton County, Georgia, at age 39.

Her obituary identifies her husband, Arthur M. Freeman, and daughter, Elizabeth Renea Griffin. It also names her surviving siblings: Ollie Singletary, Evelyn Webb, Ida Mae Seacrest, Dorothy Hines, Deloris Morgan, James Herrin, and Billy Herrin.

Shelby was buried at Woodlawn Cemetery in Perry, Taylor County, Florida.
