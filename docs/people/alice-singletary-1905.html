Biography

Alice Singletary was born around 1905 in Florida. She was the daughter of John V. Singletary and Mary Bryant Singletary.

The 1910 United States Federal Census lists Alice, approximately five years old, living with her parents and siblings in Lafayette County, Florida.

    1920 United States Federal Census, Lafayette County, Florida, Enumeration District 86, sheet 15A, lines 20-24, enumerated January 28-29, 1920.
