Biography

Zilpha Williams was born around 1825 in Georgia, according to family research. She was a daughter of John Williams and Nancy Smith and a sister of Hezekiah Ponder Williams.

Zilpha married Joseph J. Cohen. In the 1850 United States Census, she appears as Zelpha Cohen, age 28, in the household of John and Nancy Williams in Ware County, Georgia. A five-year-old child named William Cohen was also recorded in the household.

Family research indicates that Zilpha died in 1905. Her exact birth and death dates have not yet been verified.
