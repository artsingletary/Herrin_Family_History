Biography

Malachi M. Matthews was born around 1840. His Florida death certificate identifies his birthplace as Thomas County, Georgia, and his father as David Mathis.

Malachi married Mary Williams on October 27, 1870, in Madison County, Florida. Their marriage is documented in the county marriage register.

Historical records use several variations of the family surname, including Matthews, Mathews, and Mathis. Malachi died on August 23, 1924, in Madison County, Florida.

Military records identify a soldier named Malachi Matthews, also recorded as Malicah or Malachai Matthews, who served as a private in Company D of the 2nd Florida Cavalry (Union) during the American Civil War. A federal pension index records an application filed in Florida on July 19, 1890, under application number 816825, with pension certificate number 665746. The matching name and military unit provide strong evidence connecting these records, although additional documentation is needed to confirm that the soldier was the same Malachi Matthews who lived in Madison County, Florida.

In the spring of 2026, a descendant visited Mount Olive Cemetery in Madison County, Florida, searching for Malachi's grave. Although his death certificate identifies Mount Olive Cemetery as his burial location, no headstone could be located. During the visit, a local resident recalled that the cemetery once contained wooden grave markers that had disappeared over time. It is possible that Malachi's grave was originally marked with wood, although this has not been confirmed.
