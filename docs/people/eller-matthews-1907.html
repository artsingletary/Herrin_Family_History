Biography

Eller Matthews was born on September 13, 1907. She was the daughter of William M. Matthews, also known by the nickname Bunk.

Eller married Glover Mathis on December 16, 1932, in Mayo, Florida. Glover was the son of Daniel M. Mathis and Mary Frances Darling.

William M. Matthews's 1959 obituary identifies Mrs. Glover Mathis as his daughter. Eller's 1980 Florida death certificate identifies her father as Bunk Matthews, the nickname used by William M. Matthews. These records provide supporting evidence of their father-daughter relationship.

Eller died on December 16, 1980, in Sarasota, Florida. Her death certificate identifies Woodlawn Cemetery in Miami as her burial location.
