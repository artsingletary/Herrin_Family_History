Biography

Viola Singletary was born around 1908 in Florida. She was the daughter of John V. Singletary and Mary Bryant Singletary.

The 1910 United States Federal Census lists Viola, approximately two years old, living with her parents and siblings in Lafayette County, Florida.

  1. 1920 United States Census - George W. Singletary Household
    Type: Census

    The 1920 United States Census records George W. Singletary and his wife, Lela M. Singletary, living in Precinct No. 2, Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida. Their five-month-old son, Dewey, appears as "Duie" in the census. George's younger sisters Alice Geneva and Viola Singletary were also living in the household. The census page was enumerated on January 28 and 29, 1920.
