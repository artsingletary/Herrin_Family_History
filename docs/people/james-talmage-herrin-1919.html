Biography

James Talmage Herrin Sr. was born on November 12, 1919, in Pearson, Atkinson County, Georgia.

He was a son of William Levi Herrin and Laura Etta Palmer. He died on February 8, 1986, in Lake City, Columbia County, Florida.
