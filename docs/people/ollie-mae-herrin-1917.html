Biography

Ollie Mae Herrin was born on September 6, 1917, in Homerville, Clinch County, Georgia, the oldest child of William Levi Herrin and Laura Etta Palmer. As a young child she was recorded as Ollie Clayton, a name she did not like and later replaced in everyday use with Ollie Mae. By 1940, contemporary records were consistently identifying her as Ollie Mae.

Ollie spent her childhood in south Georgia as her parents' family steadily grew. The 1920 census found her with her parents and younger brother James Talmage in Clinch County. By 1930, the family was living in Pearson, Atkinson County, and included six children. During the 1930s the Herrin family moved from Georgia to Lafayette County, Florida, where the 1940 census recorded Ollie Mae, then 22, still living with her parents and younger siblings.

On June 21, 1940, Ollie Mae married Dewey Singletary in Lafayette County, Florida. They established their home in the Mayo area. The 1945 Florida State Census records Ollie and Dewey with their four-year-old son Arthur. Dewey was working as a laborer, while Ollie was recorded as a housewife. By 1950, their family had grown with the birth of another son, Emory, and Dewey was working as an automobile mechanic.

In 1955, Ollie Mae and Dewey experienced the loss of their son James Loyd, who was stillborn at Tallahassee Memorial Hospital. Surviving records disagree slightly on the exact date, with evidence indicating September 24 or September 25.

Ollie Mae and Dewey eventually made their home in the Perry area. Dewey died in 1973, leaving Ollie a widow. Ollie remained in Perry for the rest of her life.

Ollie Mae Singletary died at Doctors Memorial Hospital in Perry on June 7, 1992, at the age of 74. She was buried at Woodlawn Cemetery in Perry, where other members of the Herrin and Singletary families are also buried.
