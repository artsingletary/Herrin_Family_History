Biography

William M. Matthews was born on December 16, 1883, according to his World War I draft registration. His Florida death certificate records the same month and day but gives his birth year as 1882.

William appears to be the son of Malachi M. Matthews and Mary Williams. Census records identify a William or Willie in Malachi's household, although the recorded ages are inconsistent. His death certificate identifies his father as Miliki Matthews and his mother as Mary Adams, a discrepancy that remains unresolved.

On September 12, 1918, William registered for the World War I draft while living in Lee, Madison County, Florida. He worked in sawmilling for the West Yellow Pine Company in Weston, Florida. He identified Daniel M. Matthews of Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, as his nearest relative. This provides additional evidence connecting William to Daniel M. Mathis.

William died on December 4, 1959, in Chattahoochee, Gadsden County, Florida. His obituary in the Tallahassee Democrat reported that he was a lifelong Madison resident and that his funeral arrangements included burial at Oak Ridge Cemetery.
