  1. 1920 U.S. Federal Census — Daniel M. Mathis Family
    Type: Census

    1920 United States Federal Census, Lafayette County, Florida, Precinct No. 3, sheet 7B, lines 86–96. The household includes Dan M. Mathis (47), wife Mary (37), and their eight children: Claude (19), Lela (17), Rube (15), Frank (13), Roy (11), Glover (10), Shirley (6), and Jessie M. (2). Also residing in the household was Ella Truluck (15), identified as a niece. Ella was the daughter of Silas P. Truluck and Annie E. Mathis, Daniel's sister and a daughter of Malachi M. Mathis and Mary Williams.

  2. 1932 Marriage Certificate - Glover Mathis and Eller Matthews
    Type: marriage

    Marriage record from Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, documenting the marriage of Glover Mathis and Eller Mathis on December 16, 1932. The record identifies the couple by their names as written at the time of marriage. Their subsequent Florida death certificates confirm their marriage and identify Eller Matthews as Glover's surviving wife in 1973.

  3. 1973 Florida Death Certificate - Glover Mathis
    Type: death

    Florida death certificate documenting the death of Glover Mathis on December 12, 1973, in West Palm Beach, Palm Beach County, Florida. His birth date is recorded as November 16, 1911. His father is identified as Daniel Mathis and his mother as Mary Dowling, a surname variation associated with Mary Frances Darling in family records. His surviving wife is identified as Eller Matthews. Glover was a retired truck driver and was buried at Airline Church Cemetery in Lafayette County, Florida.
