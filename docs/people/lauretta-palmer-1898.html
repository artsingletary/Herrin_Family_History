Biography

Lauretta Palmer was born on June 25, 1898, in Pearson, Atkinson County, Georgia.

She was recorded as Lauretta Palmer in the 1900 census when she was a young child. In later

records, her name appears as Laura, Laura E.,and Laura Etta Palmer
