Biography

James Loyd Singletary was the son of Dewey Singletary and Ollie Mae Herrin. He was stillborn in Tallahassee, Florida, in September 1955. Surviving records disagree slightly on the exact date, with evidence indicating September 24 or September 25.

James Loyd was buried at Airline Baptist Church Cemetery in Mayo, Florida.
