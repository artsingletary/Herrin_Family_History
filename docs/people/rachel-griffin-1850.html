Biography

In August 1850, Rachel Griffin was enumerated in District 81 of Thomas County, Georgia, in the household of John and Eliza Griffin. The census recorded Rachel as five months old, suggesting a birth around March 1850. Her 1935 death certificate independently identifies John Griffin as her father, providing additional evidence connecting Rachel to this household.

On October 14, 1869, Rachel Griffin married Simon P. Singletary in Decatur County, Georgia. The marriage is documented in the original county marriage register. Her husband appears in other historical records as Silas P. Singletary.

In 1870, Rachel was living in Decatur County, Georgia, in the household of Jacob Singletary. The federal census recorded her as eighteen years old. Her presence alongside Silas Singletary, whom she had married the previous year, supports the identification of this household.

By 1900, Rachel and Silas were living in Thomas County, Georgia. The federal census identified Rachel as Silas's wife and recorded several children in their household, including Emma P., Mary, Robert, and Dollie Singletary.

Rachel's husband, Silas P. Singletary, died on January 10, 1915, according to his burial record. Rachel survived him by approximately twenty years.

Rachel died on June 28, 1935, in Ochlocknee, Thomas County, Georgia. Her death certificate identifies her father as John Griffin and provides a mother's maiden name that requires further verification. The certificate records chronic nephritis and senility as causes of death. Rachel was buried on June 29, 1935, at Springhill Cemetery near Ochlocknee.
