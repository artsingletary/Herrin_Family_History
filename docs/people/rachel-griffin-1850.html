Biography

Rachel Griffin was born in Georgia around March 1850. In August 1850, she was enumerated in District 81 of Thomas County, Georgia, in the household of John and Eliza Griffin. The census recorded her age as five months. Her 1935 death certificate independently identifies John Griffin as her father, providing additional evidence connecting Rachel to this household.

On October 14, 1869, Rachel married Simon P. Singletary in Decatur County, Georgia. Their marriage is documented in the original county marriage register. Her husband appears in other historical records as Silas P. Singletary and S. P. Singletary.

In 1870, Rachel was living in Decatur County, Georgia, in the household of Jacob Singletary. The federal census recorded her as eighteen years old. Her presence alongside Silas Singletary, whom she had married the previous year, provides additional evidence connecting the marriage record to the Singletary family.

By June 1900, Rachel and Silas were living in Thomas County, Georgia. The federal census identified Rachel as Silas's wife and recorded four children in their household: Emma P., Mary, Robert, and Dollie Singletary.

Rachel's husband, Silas P. Singletary, died on January 10, 1915, according to his burial record. He was buried at Springhill Christian Church Cemetery near Ochlocknee, Thomas County, Georgia.

In 1916, Rachel applied for a Confederate widow's pension in Thomas County, Georgia, based on her late husband's military service. Her application, preserved in the Georgia Archives, identifies her husband as S. P. Singletary and provides information about their marriage. The pension documents also contain a handwritten statement concerning differences in recollections of the couple's marriage. Their marriage is independently documented in the original Decatur County marriage register dated October 14, 1869.

Rachel died on June 28, 1935, in Ochlocknee, Thomas County, Georgia. Her Georgia death certificate records her age as ninety-five, suggesting a birth around 1840. However, the 1850 federal census recorded her as five months old, suggesting a birth around March 1850. The earlier census provides stronger evidence for her approximate birth year, although the discrepancy remains unresolved.

Rachel's death certificate identifies her father as John Griffin and records a mother's maiden name that has not yet been conclusively deciphered. The certificate lists chronic nephritis and senility among the causes of death. Rachel was buried on June 29, 1935, at Springhill Cemetery near Ochlocknee, Georgia.
