Biography

Emma Singletary Evans was born on August 24, 1895, in Lafayette County, Florida, the daughter of Reverend Jacob A. Singletary (1852–1931) and Ida Alla Miller Singletary (1873–1945). Her birth date is recorded on her gravestone and in her 1984 funeral memorial. Her obituary identifies her as a native of Lafayette County, where her family lived during her childhood.

Emma grew up in Lafayette County, Florida, where her father worked as a farmer and was also identified as a minister. She appears in the 1900 United States Federal Census in the household of Jacob and Ida Singletary, along with her siblings Eliza E., Sarah, William Robert, and Henry Singletary.

The 1910 United States Federal Census again records Emma living with her parents in Lafayette County. The household included several of her siblings, among them Sallie, Robert, Henry, Giddy, and Jamie. The family remained closely associated with the rural communities of Lafayette County and Bethel Creek Baptist Church.

Emma married Willie Evans, who was born on July 9, 1887, and died on October 7, 1971, according to their shared gravestone. The date and location of their marriage have not yet been established. Additional census and marriage records may help document their early married life.

Emma and Willie Evans had at least three children identified in Emma's 1984 obituary and funeral memorial: Grace Evans, later Grace Lassiter; Ernest Evans; and Fred Evans. At the time of Emma's death, Grace was living in Palatka, Florida, while Ernest and Fred were living in Jacksonville, Florida. The obituary also records ten grandchildren and twenty-five great-grandchildren.

Emma was identified as a surviving sister in the February 1961 obituary of her brother William Robert Singletary. That obituary listed her as Mrs. Emma Evans, along with her sisters Eliza Bell and Sally Spies and brothers Henry, Giddy, Jamie, and Jasper Singletary.

During her later years, Emma lived in Palatka, Putnam County, Florida. Her obituary states that she had resided there for approximately thirteen years before her death, suggesting that she moved to the area around 1971. She was a member of Tabernacle Baptist Church in Palatka.

Emma died on Saturday, April 28, 1984, at Putnam Memorial Nursing Home in Palatka, Florida, following a long illness. She was eighty-eight years old. Her obituary, published in The Florida Times-Union on April 30, 1984, identifies her as a native of Lafayette County and a longtime resident of Palatka.

Emma's obituary identified her surviving children as Grace Lassiter of Palatka and Ernest and Fred Evans of Jacksonville. Her surviving siblings were identified as Sallie Spies of Live Oak and brothers Giddy and Sammy Singletary of Mayo and Jasper Singletary of Live Oak. Her funeral memorial identifies the brother listed as Sammy in the newspaper as Jammie Singletary, a spelling consistent with the Jamie Singletary found in earlier family records. The available evidence suggests these references concern the same brother, although the variation in names should be preserved in the historical record.

Funeral services were scheduled for Monday, April 30, 1984, at 3:00 p.m. at Westwood Baptist Church in Live Oak, Florida, with Reverend Lorace Cothron officiating. Harris Funeral Home of Live Oak handled the arrangements. Emma was buried at Live Oak Cemetery in Live Oak, Suwannee County, Florida.

Emma shares a gravestone with her husband, Willie Evans, at Live Oak Cemetery. The marker records Willie's birth on July 9, 1887, and death on October 7, 1971, and Emma's birth on August 24, 1895, and death on April 28, 1984. The shared gravestone provides important evidence of their marriage and burial location.
