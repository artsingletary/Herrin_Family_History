Biography

Ida Alla Miller was born on May 28, 1873, according to her gravestone. Her birthplace and parents have not yet been established.

On April 16, 1890, Ida married Jacob A. Singletary in Lafayette County, Florida. Their original marriage record identifies the bride as Ida Alla Miller and the groom as Jacob A. Singletary. The marriage ceremony was performed by D. H. Ward, and the license was filed for record on April 26, 1890.

Ida was sixteen years old at the time of her marriage, based on the birth date recorded on her gravestone. Jacob was thirty-eight years old.

Her husband, Jacob A. Singletary, was a minister and is identified as Reverend Jacob Singletary on their shared gravestone.

Ida died on October 16, 1945. Her gravestone identifies her as Ally Ida Singletary, a variation of the name recorded in her marriage record. She is commemorated alongside her husband at Bell's Mill Creek Cemetery, also known as Bethel Creek Baptist Church Cemetery, in Day, Lafayette County, Florida.
