Biography

David Brewer Webb, known as Buddy, was born on June 22, 1941.

David was married to Evelyn Herrin (1934–2011). Florida divorce records document their divorce in Taylor County on June 6, 1975. Whether they subsequently remarried has not yet been established.

David died on February 6, 2007. He and Evelyn are commemorated on a shared gravestone at Waters Memorial Gardens in Steinhatchee, Taylor County, Florida.

The gravestone identifies four children: Stevie, Ricky, Connie Ray, and Debra. Their individual parentage has not yet been established.
