Biography

Linda Miller was born December 13, 1953, in Georgia, the daughter of Charles Alton Miller Sr. and Mary Lou Herrin. Her mother was a daughter of William Levi Herrin and Laura Etta Palmer. Linda had a brother, Robert B. Miller, known as Robbie, and a stepbrother, Charles Miller Jr.

Linda's name appears differently in surviving family records. Her mother's 1972 obituary identifies her as Laura Linda Miller, while her 1973 marriage announcement and marriage record identify her as Linda Louise Miller. Family recollections suggest her original name may have been Linda Laura Miller. Her gravestone identifies her simply as Linda Miller. Her full birth name has not yet been established.

Linda graduated from the Florida School for the Deaf and Blind in St. Augustine, Florida, and later attended Florida State University. Both educational institutions were identified in the newspaper announcement of her marriage.

On July 29, 1973, Linda married Jeffrey Phillip Smith at Sierra-Norwood Baptist Church. Their marriage was announced in The Miami Herald on July 30, 1973. A separate marriage record gives August 5, 1973, and the reason for the difference between the recorded dates has not yet been established.

Linda and Jeffrey divorced October 30, 1989, in Broward County, Florida. According to family recollections, Jeffrey was Linda's only husband, and she was unmarried at the time of her death. No children have been identified from their marriage.

Linda died January 8, 2020, in Florida, at the age of 66. She was buried at Woodlawn Cemetery in Perry, Taylor County, Florida, beside her mother, Mary Lou Herrin. Her gravestone bears the inscription 'Much Love.'
