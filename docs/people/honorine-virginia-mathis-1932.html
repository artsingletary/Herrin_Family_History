Biography

Honorine Virginia Mathis was born October 5, 1932, in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, the daughter of Frank Mathis and Mary Virginia Allen. She was a sister of Joseph Henry Mathis.

Frank Mathis's 1969 obituary identifies his daughter as Honorine Barber, living in Camilla, Georgia. Later records identify her as Honorine Virginia Mathis Grimsley. The details and dates of her marriages have not yet been established.

Honorine died May 27, 2005, in Camilla, Mitchell County, Georgia, at age 72. She was buried at Mount Pleasant Cemetery in Chattahoochee, Gadsden County, Florida, where her father was also buried.
