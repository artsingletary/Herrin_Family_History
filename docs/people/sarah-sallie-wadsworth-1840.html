Biography

Sarah Wadsworth, also known as Sallie, was born on March 19, 1840. She was previously married to Greenberry Blanton, who is believed to have died during the Civil War. This account of his death has not yet been independently verified.

Sarah subsequently married Everett Darling in Madison County, Florida. Their marriage is documented by a surviving marriage license.

Sarah was the mother of Mary Frances Darling. She died on March 30, 1908, at the age of 68.
