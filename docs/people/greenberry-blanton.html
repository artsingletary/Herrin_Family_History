Biography

Greenberry Blanton, also recorded as Green Berry or Berry Blanton, was born May 25, 1829, according to the published family history Aldermans in America. That publication identifies his parents as David Blanton and Nancy Goff.

Greenberry married Sarah M. Wadsworth, also known as Sallie Wadsworth, on December 6, 1857, in Madison County, Florida.

In the 1860 United States Census, Greenberry was living in Lowndes County, Georgia, with his wife Sarah and two children, Emma E. Blanton and William T. Blanton. The census recorded his age as 31 and his birthplace as North Carolina.

Greenberry's whereabouts after the 1860 census remain uncertain. Aldermans in America reports that he died around 1863, but this date has not been independently verified.

A Confederate military index identifies a G. B. Blanton serving as a private in Company H, 26th Georgia Infantry. Although the name and geographic connection make this a possible match, no evidence has yet established that the soldier was Greenberry Blanton.

Sarah subsequently married Everett Darling on March 28, 1879, in Madison County, Florida. The 1880 census records a four-year-old girl named Liddy A. Blanton as Everett's stepdaughter. Liddy's biological father has not been established, and her relationship to Greenberry remains uncertain.
