Biography

Margaret Fussell was born on November 4, 1913, in Georgia. She was the daughter of Dr. Thomas Daniel Fussell and Sarah Redman Fussell, as identified in the obituary of her daughter, Stella Emily Secreast Osborn.

Margaret married Lawrence Monroe Secreast, who was born on February 18, 1912, in Lenoir, North Carolina. Lawrence had previously been married to Ruth A. Sherrill, whom he divorced on November 22, 1946, in St. Johns County, Florida. The exact date and location of Margaret and Lawrence's marriage have not yet been established.

Margaret and Lawrence had a daughter, Stella Emily Secreast, born on January 13, 1948, in Macon, Bibb County, Georgia.

On April 26, 1950, the United States federal census recorded Margaret, age 36, living with her husband Lawrence, age 38, and their daughter Stella, age two, in Pensacola, Escambia County, Florida. Lawrence was employed as a cabinet maker in a furniture shop. The census recorded Margaret and Stella as having been born in Georgia.

Margaret and Lawrence's marriage ended sometime before Lawrence married Ida Mae Herrin Sullivan on April 5, 1959. The date and circumstances of the end of Margaret and Lawrence's marriage have not yet been established.

Margaret died on January 26, 1984, at the age of 70. She was buried at Sharon Baptist Church Cemetery in Telfair County, Georgia.
