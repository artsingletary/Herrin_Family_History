Biography

Marie Richmond Evans was born around 1926 in Louisiana. Her parents and exact date of birth have not yet been established.

By April 1950, Marie was married to Frederick (Fred) Evans, son of Willie Evans and Emma Singletary Evans. The couple lived in Suwannee County, Florida. The 1950 United States federal census recorded Marie, age 24, with Fred and four children: Charles Richmond, age 10; Donald Richmond, age 8; Freddie Evans, age 2; and Willie Evans, age 1. Charles and Donald were identified as Fred's stepsons. It is not yet known whether Richmond was Marie's maiden name or a surname from an earlier marriage.

On June 7, 1952, Marie and her family were enjoying an outing along the Suwannee River near Suwannee Springs, Florida. According to contemporary newspaper reports, Donald Ray entered the water and was caught in a strong undertow. His older brother Charles attempted to rescue him, followed by Marie. All three drowned. Fred also entered the river in an attempt to save his family and was rescued by his brother-in-law, Owen Lassiter.

The tragedy was reported in newspapers, including The St. Augustine Record on June 8, 1952. Marie was approximately 26 years old at the time of her death. Her burial location has not yet been established.

Sources

  1. 1952 Newspaper Article – Mother and Two Sons Drowned in Suwannee River
    Type: newspaper

    Article titled 'Mother And Two Sons Drowned In Suwannee River,' published in The St. Augustine Record on June 8, 1952. The Associated Press report, datelined Live Oak, Florida, June 7, describes the drowning of Mrs. Fred Evans, identified through family research as Marie Richmond Evans, and her sons Donald Ray Evans, age 10, and Charles Evans, age 12, during a family fishing and swimming outing on the Suwannee River. According to the article, Donald Ray entered the river and was caught in a strong undertow. Charles attempted to help his brother and was also pulled under. Their mother entered the water to rescue the boys and drowned as well. Fred Evans, who had been fishing nearby, entered the river in an attempt to save his family but was rescued by his brother-in-law, Owen Lassiter. Lassiter reported that he had briefly held all four family members but could not pull them from the strong current. The article identifies the victims by the surname Evans; the 1950 federal census separately records Charles and Donald with the surname Richmond and identifies them as Fred's stepsons. The newspaper does not provide Marie's given name. The report establishes that the tragedy occurred on June 7, 1952.
