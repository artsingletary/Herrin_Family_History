Biography

Richard Mathis, known as Ricky, is the son of Freddy Ray Mathis (1938–1967) and Evelyn Herrin (1934–2011).

Ricky grew up in the Perry, Florida, area and attended Taylor County High School. He appears in school yearbook records from 1977 and 1979.

Ricky is the brother of Connie Ray Mathis.
