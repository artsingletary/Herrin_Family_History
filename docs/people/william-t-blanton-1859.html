Biography

William T. Blanton was born around 1859, the son of Greenberry Blanton and Sarah M. Wadsworth.

He appears in the 1860 United States Census in Lowndes County, Georgia, at six months of age.

Family research indicates that William died in 1881. The source confirming his death has not yet been added to the archive.
