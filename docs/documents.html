Type: newspaper

Date: 1952-06-08

Location: Live Oak, Suwannee County, Florida

Article titled 'Mother And Two Sons Drowned In Suwannee River,' published in The St. Augustine Record on June 8, 1952. According to the article, Donald Ray entered the river and was caught in a strong undertow. Charles attempted to help his brother and was also pulled under. Their mother entered the water to rescue the boys and drowned as well. Fred Evans, who had been fishing nearby, entered the river in an attempt to save his family but was rescued by his brother-in-law, Owen Lassiter. Lassiter reported that he had briefly held all four family members but could not pull them from the strong current. The article identifies the victims by the surname Evans; the 1950 federal census separately records Charles and Donald with the surname Richmond and identifies them as Fred's stepsons. The newspaper does not provide Marie's given name. The report establishes that the tragedy occurred on June 7, 1952.
